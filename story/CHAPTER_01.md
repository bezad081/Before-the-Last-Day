# Chapter 1 — The Morning That Didn't End

**Format:** linear story, direct-control 2D side-scroller  
**Location:** a lively city street leading toward Central Station  
**Purpose:** establish the protagonist's ordinary life, introduce Mira, and let the first signs of crisis interrupt everyday routine.

## Story beats

1. **Opening — 8:07 AM.** Warm light, commuters, shops opening. The protagonist is late to meet Mira.
2. **Small banter.** Mira teases the protagonist about being late. Keep dialogue human and lightly funny.
3. **First disturbance.** A distant siren passes; people notice, then return to their routines.
4. **Public alert.** A phone notification reports a disturbance near Central Station.
5. **Atmosphere shift.** A shopkeeper closes their shutters; the street becomes subtly less welcoming.
6. **Objective.** The protagonist continues toward the station.
7. **Chapter ending.** The protagonist reaches the entrance as distant panic breaks through the morning noise. Cut before showing the full disaster.

## Tone and pacing

Begin colorful, warm, and lightly comedic. Add unease through sound, background behavior, and small environmental details rather than sudden exposition. Keep the story linear. Player movement should provide presence and pacing, not change the canonical plot.

## Prototype note

The first browser build currently implements the intro, dialogue sequence, street traversal, and a placeholder chapter-end beat. Some outlined story events still need custom art, sound, scripted background actors, and cinematic transitions.
